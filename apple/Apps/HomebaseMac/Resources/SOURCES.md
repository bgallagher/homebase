# Theme sources

Third-party themes in `themes.json` come from the open **iTerm2-Color-Schemes** collection
(https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes), MIT licensed. Each scheme was created by its own author;
the collection's `CREDITS.md` names them, and the matching lines are quoted below where the name matches.
Homebase Dark and Homebase Light are our own.

Regenerate with `tools/import-themes`. Do not script-download from terminalcolors.com: its `robots.txt`
disallows `/downloads/`.
